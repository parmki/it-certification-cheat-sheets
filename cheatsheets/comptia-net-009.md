## 🪜 CompTIA 7-Step Troubleshooting Methodology
1. **Identify the problem**: Gather info, question users, identify symptoms, duplicate if possible.
2. **Establish a theory of probable cause**: Question the obvious, consider multiple approaches (top-to-bottom / divide-and-conquer).
3. **Test the theory to determine cause**: Once confirmed, proceed. If not confirmed, re-establish theory or escalate.
4. **Establish a plan of action**: Determine steps and identify potential side effects before executing.
5. **Implement the solution or escalate**: Make one change at a time.
6. **Verify full system functionality**: Implement preventive measures where applicable.
7. **Document findings, actions, and outcomes**: Ensure complete historical audit logs.
